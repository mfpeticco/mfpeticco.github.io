Outreach

“Robotic Hands & Wrists” — Discover Mechanical Engineering, MIT

August 2025

A research talk to roughly 30 incoming MIT first-years on why robotic hands and wrists are the current frontier in robotics: how hands are categorized, a century of designs from early prostheses to modern dexterous hands, and today’s learning-driven return of highly actuated designs. I closed on my own work with DexWrist.

Newburgh Free Academy P-TECH — Summer 2025

July 2025

I returned to Newburgh Free Academy’s P-TECH program, the one I came up through, to talk with incoming students about getting from a P-TECH associate’s degree to engineering research, framed around what I would tell my own ninth-grade self. One slide read “Tip #1: Stay busy, take every opportunity.”
