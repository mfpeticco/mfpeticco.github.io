I am a third-year PhD student in MIT EECS, working in the [Improbable AI Lab](https://improbableai.com/) under [Dr. Pulkit Agrawal](https://people.csail.mit.edu/pulkitag/). My research focuses on designing and building highly dexterous robotic manipulation hardware to be controlled through learning-based algorithms.

Previously, I designed the Starship Block 2 payload door at SpaceX, worked on robotic snakes for icy-moon exploration at NASA JPL, and did aerospace robotics research at Caltech. I earned my bachelor's in mechanical engineering and aerospace engineering from Caltech in 2023.

In my free time I like to work on personal engineering projects, do aerial photography, tinker with drones, build Lego, and gym. I am from Newburgh, NY.
