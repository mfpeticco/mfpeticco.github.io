As mechanical lead of Team Domino for Caltech's ME134 class, I designed, built, and helped program a robotic arm powered by six HEBI motors, ROS, Python, and OpenCV. It places standard-sized dominoes on a five-by-five-foot table along a user-drawn path. I designed the 5-DoF arm, the gripper assembly, and the automatic domino dispenser.
