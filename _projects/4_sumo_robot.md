For Caltech's ME72ab capstone design competition, teams built and fought three robots each: wheeled RC, wheeled autonomous, and a walking RC robot. As mechanical design and drivetrain lead, I developed a robot capable of pushing over 100 kg at a weight of 3 kg, using two electric-skateboard-sized motors and over 180 kg of magnetic downforce. My team placed **first** in the 37th Annual Engineering Design Competition.

## Recap Video

Every year, Caltech's ME72 capstone design project class has a unique competition amongst its enrolled students to build and compete with robots designed and developed throughout the course of two terms. When I took the class, the competition was sumo robots, which was a deceptively simple-sounding engineering project that ended up being very involved with many nuances. Here is a recap video I made summarizing the whole two-term experience of building and fighting sumo robots!
